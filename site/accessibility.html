Accessibility

Last updated October 31, 2026

We want everyone to be able to browse the menu, order and plan events with The Goose. This site aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA.

What we’ve done

  • Text and controls meet color-contrast guidelines; touch targets are at least 44×44 pixels.
  • Full keyboard navigation with visible focus indicators and a “skip to content” link.
  • Form fields have visible labels, helpful hints and clear error messages.
  • Animations respect your device’s “reduce motion” setting.
  • Images and illustrations include text alternatives or are hidden from screen readers when decorative.
  • Pages work when zoomed to 200% and on phone-sized screens.

Need help?

If anything on this site is hard to use, call us at (610) 868-0176 and we’ll take your order by phone. Please also let us know what didn’t work so we can fix it.
