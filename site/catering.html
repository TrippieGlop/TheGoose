How catering works

  1. Pick your spread

    Choose trays, a cold buffet, or let us build a hot buffet for your headcount.

  2. Tell us the details

    Date, time, number of guests, pickup or drop-off — send an inquiry or call.

  3. We confirm & quote

    The Goosemen will confirm your order and final price before the big day.

  4. Eat like a legend

    Buffets include plates, napkins, utensils & tablecloth.
