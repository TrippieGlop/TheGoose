Our story

A family deli on Fourth Street.

Ask any Lehigh student where to eat, and they’ll point you down Fourth Street.

Old-timers still call it the Goosey Gander Deli. It’s a family-run sandwich shop just off Lehigh’s campus, and for decades students, professors and South Side neighbors have lined up here for lunch. Student groups even team up with the deli for “Keep the Goose Open Late” nights to raise money for their causes.
