Our story

A South Side tradition, one hoagie at a time.

Ask any Lehigh student where to eat, and they’ll point you down Fourth Street.

The Goose — the Goosey Gander Deli to old-timers — is a family-run sandwich shop just off Lehigh’s campus. Fresh-sliced meats, generous builds and friendly faces behind the counter have made it a lunch ritual for students, professors, families and South Side neighbors for decades. Student groups even team up with the deli for “Keep the Goose Open Late” nights to raise money for their causes.
