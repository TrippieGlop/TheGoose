Privacy Policy

Last updated October 31, 2026

The Goosemen Inc. (“The Goose,” “we,” “us”) runs this website and our deli at 102 W. Fourth Street, Bethlehem, PA 18015. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account & ordering: name, street address, city, state, ZIP, phone, email, username, account type (e.g., student), and your order history and instructions.
  • Catering & event inquiries: name, phone, email, event date, guest count and any details you share.
  • Communications: messages you send us and whether you opted in to coupons and event emails.

Information collected automatically

Our hosting provider records standard technical logs (such as IP address, browser type, pages requested and timestamps) to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. This site does not use advertising trackers or sell-your-data style analytics.

2. How we use information

  • To create and manage your account and to prepare, confirm and hand off your orders.
  • To respond to catering and Gander Room inquiries and provide quotes.
  • To send coupons and event notices only if you opt in.
  • To keep the website secure, prevent fraud and fix problems.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

3. Payments

Card payments for online orders are processed by our ordering system’s payment processor. Your full card number is sent directly to that processor over an encrypted connection and is never stored on this website or seen by our staff. We may receive limited details such as the card type, last four digits and whether the payment succeeded, so we can match payments to orders. We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express.

4. How we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that help us operate — website hosting, our online ordering platform, payment processing and email — who may use it only to provide services to us.
  • Legal and safety recipients when required by law or to protect the rights, property or safety of our customers, staff or others.
  • A successor if the business is sold or transferred, subject to this policy.

5. Cookies & local storage

This site does not set advertising or tracking cookies. It uses your browser’s local storage for small conveniences only — for example, remembering that you dismissed an announcement or which golden eggs you’ve found. That information stays on your device, is never sent to us, and you can clear it anytime in your browser settings. Our separate online ordering system may use essential cookies to keep you logged in and your cart working.

6. Third-party services

Some pages load content from third parties, which may receive your IP address when you view them: Google Fonts (typefaces), Google Maps (the embedded map), and links to Instagram, Facebook and X. Their own privacy policies govern how they handle data.

7. Security

The site is served only over HTTPS with modern security headers, and forms include protections against spam. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a unique password for your account and tell us right away if you suspect unauthorized use.

8. Retention

We keep account information while your account is active and order records as long as needed for business, tax and legal purposes. Inquiry details are kept only as long as needed to respond and plan your event.

9. Your choices & rights

  • Email: unsubscribe from marketing using the link in any email or by contacting us. We’ll still send order-related messages.
  • Access, correction & deletion: contact us to review, update or delete your account information, subject to records we must keep by law.
  • Do Not Track / Global Privacy Control: because we don’t track you across sites or sell data, there’s nothing to opt out of — but we honor these signals.

10. Children

This site isn’t directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we’ll delete it.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. We’ll change the “last updated” date above and, for significant changes, post a notice on the site.
